A Ho Chi Minh City cancer-awareness survey

3 in 4 respondents reported a breast or liver cancer warning sign.

Among 170 self-reported responses across 19 Ho Chi Minh City districts, most respondents reported at least one warning sign — and the majority of those reported breast and liver signs together. The analysis is descriptive. Reported warning signs are not to be interpreted as cancer diagnoses, and the visualizations should not be read as causal evidence.

What this story is — and is not.

This story is based on 170 self-reported survey responses from Ho Chi Minh City. Reported warning signs are not cancer diagnoses. District rates and lifestyle correlations describe this sample only; they do not prove cause or represent the city as a whole.
